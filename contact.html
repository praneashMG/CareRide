Quick Answers

Before reaching out, you might find the answer to your question here.

What if I have a medical emergency?

Call 911 immediately. CareRide is a Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT) service. We do not provide lights-and-sirens ambulance transport for acute medical emergencies.

Can I schedule a ride over the phone?

Absolutely. While our Family Dashboard is the easiest way to manage recurring rides, you can always call our dispatch line at (800) 555-0199 to have a coordinator book the ride for you.

How quickly do you respond to emails?

Our administrative team responds to all email inquiries within 2 hours during normal business hours (8AM - 6PM). For urgent matters outside these hours, please call our 24/7 dispatch line.

How can I apply to become a companion driver?

We are always looking for compassionate individuals with a clean driving record and a heart for seniors. Please select "Apply to be a Driver" in the contact form above to start the screening process.
