Our Mission

More Than a Ride—We Are
Caregivers on Wheels

CareRide was founded on a simple principle: seniors deserve to maintain their independence without compromising their safety, and families deserve absolute peace of mind.

Get in Touch Read Our Story

"We built the service we desperately wished existed for our own parents."

Born From a Family's Need

In 2018, our founders faced a common crisis: balancing full-time careers while trying to get their aging mother to thrice-weekly dialysis appointments. Rideshare apps were unsafe and lacked empathy, while traditional medical transport felt clinical and unreliable.

They realized that the gap in senior transportation wasn't just about vehicles—it was about care. Transportation needed to be an extension of the healthcare system and the family unit.

CareRide was born to bridge that gap. We started with a single ADA-compliant van and a commitment to treat every passenger with the dignity and patience we demanded for our own family. Today, we operate a fleet across the region, but our core philosophy remains unchanged.

Robert Sterling

Founder & CEO

The Pillars of CareRide

Everything we do, from hiring drivers to designing our Family Dashboard, is guided by these four non-negotiable principles.

Safety Above All

We never compromise on safety. From vehicle maintenance to CPR certifications and infectious disease protocols, clinical safety is our baseline.

Empathetic Care

We hire for heart. Technical skills can be taught, but genuine patience, warmth, and empathy for aging adults must come naturally to our team.

Radical Transparency

Families deserve to know where their loved ones are. We provide live GPS tracking, instant alerts, and fully transparent, flat-rate pricing.

Preserving Dignity

Aging shouldn't mean isolation. We facilitate independence by making social outings, errands, and medical care accessible and dignified.

Guided by Experts
