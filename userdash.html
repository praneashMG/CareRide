Common Questions

What if my parent needs to cancel a ride?

You can cancel free of charge up to 2 hours before pickup. After that, a $15 late-cancellation fee applies. Just call dispatch or cancel from this dashboard.

How do I change the preferred driver?

Go to My Passengers, click the edit icon on the passenger card, and update the preferred driver field. We'll try to match that driver whenever possible.

Is there a fee for extra stops?

Each additional stop beyond the route is $8. Wait time beyond 1 hour is billed at $15 per 30 minutes. All fees are shown before you confirm.

Can I track the driver in real time?

Yes. From the Track Driver tab, you'll see live progress and ETA. We also send SMS alerts at key milestones.
