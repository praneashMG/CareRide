Billing & Insurance FAQ

Does CareRide accept Medicare or Medicaid?

CareRide is primarily a private-pay service. However, some Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans or Medicaid waivers cover Non-Emergency Medical Transportation. We provide detailed receipts that you can submit to your provider for reimbursement if you have eligible coverage.

How does payment work for family members?

Through the Family Dashboard, you can safely store a credit or debit card on file. Rides are automatically charged upon completion. You can also split billing between multiple family members (e.g., siblings sharing the cost of a parent's transport).

What is your cancellation policy?

We understand that medical appointments change. You can cancel any ride up to 12 hours in advance with absolutely no penalty. Cancellations made within 12 hours of the pickup time are subject to a 50% cancellation fee to compensate our drivers.

Do I need to tip the driver?

CareRide is a fully cashless, no-tipping-required service. We pay our companions a premium living wage. If you wish to show extraordinary gratitude, you may add a tip through the app post-ride, but it is never expected or solicited.
