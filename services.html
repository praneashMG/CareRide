The Companion Difference

We are not a taxi service. Our drivers are trained caregivers who provide physical assistance and emotional support.

True Door-Through-Door Care

We never wait at the curb and honk. Our companions walk into your home, help with coats and locks, ensure you are safely seated in the vehicle, and escort you entirely inside your destination.

Medical Note-Taking

With family permission, our companions can sit in on medical consultations to take notes and ask clarifying questions on your behalf.

Live Family Updates

Family members receive SMS alerts and can view the dashboard when the ride starts, when the patient arrives at the doctor, and when they are safely home.

Dementia Training

Drivers receive specialized training to communicate effectively and calmly with passengers experiencing cognitive decline or Alzheimer's.

Vetted & Insured

Strict 10-point background checks, routine drug testing, clean driving records, and comprehensive liability insurance for every trip.

Specialized Care Programs

Certain situations require a higher level of attention. We have developed tailored protocols for specific medical and life circumstances to ensure maximum safety.

Consult a Care Coordinator

Vision & Mobility Impaired

Drivers act as sighted guides, offering arm support and verbally narrating surroundings, steps, and obstacles from the front door to the doctor's chair.

Day-Surgery / Post-Op Escort

Most clinics require a designated driver to stay on-site during outpatient procedures. Our companions wait in the lobby, sign the discharge papers, and safely transfer the patient home.

Long-Distance & Facility Transfers

Safe transport between cities or states when relocating a senior to a new assisted living facility or moving them closer to family members. Includes frequent comfort stops.
