How CareRide Works

We've designed our booking and riding process to be as simple and stress-free as possible for both seniors and their families.

1

Schedule Online or by Phone

Use your Family Dashboard to input pickup details, specific needs, and destination, or call our 24/7 dispatch.

2

Meet Your Companion

Your vetted driver arrives on time, assists the passenger into the vehicle, and updates the family tracker.

3

Safe Arrival & Return

We escort passengers to check-in desks or grocery aisles, wait as needed, and ensure they are safely settled back home.
